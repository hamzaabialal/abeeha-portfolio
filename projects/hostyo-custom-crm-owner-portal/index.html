The Challenge

Hostyo manages short-term rental properties across Cyprus. As the portfolio grew, keeping owners updated on bookings, expenses, payouts and guest details through spreadsheets and email threads became slow and repetitive.

The team needed one centralized system — property management software shaped around their own processes, paired with a real estate CRM for sales — that would cut manual admin work and give property owners clear, reliable information without having to ask for it.

02

What We Built

We designed and developed a custom Owner Portal — a React web application backed by Notion and deployed on Vercel — so every owner has a private, always-current view of their properties.

Owner Portal features

  • Centralized owner dashboard with balances, payouts, expenses and forecasts
  • Upcoming guest arrivals from Airbnb and Booking.com
  • Monthly financial statements and expense tracking
  • Secure, private access for each individual property owner
  • Real-time access to property-related information

03

Hostyo CRM / Admin Portal

Alongside the Owner Portal, we built a custom CRM and admin dashboard that brings Hostyo's sales pipeline and internal operations into one place, with AI automation handling the repetitive parts.

CRM & automation features

  • Automated lead collection from Facebook Ads
  • Lead management and sales tracking
  • Call recording with AI-powered transcription
  • AI-generated call summaries in Greek and English
  • WhatsApp integration
  • Google Calendar meeting synchronization
  • Digital contracts and e-signature workflows
  • Internal team chat and mobile notifications
  • Automated workflows and system health alerts

04

The Results

The new system reduced repetitive administrative work, improved communication with property owners and made the sales process faster and easier to manage.

How It Benefits The Business

Business process automation is only useful when it changes how a team works day to day. Here is what the custom CRM and owner portal changed for Hostyo.

01

Efficiency

Automated lead capture, AI call summaries and workflow alerts remove repetitive admin, so the team spends its time on owners and guests instead of spreadsheets.

02

Transparency

Owners see balances, payouts, expenses and upcoming arrivals themselves, which builds trust and reduces back-and-forth emails.

03

Faster sales

Leads from Facebook Ads arrive in the CRM automatically, and bilingual call summaries help the sales team follow up quickly and consistently.

04

Scalability

With one centralized platform, Hostyo can add properties and team members without adding the same amount of manual work.
