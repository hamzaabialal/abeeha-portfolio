01

The Challenge

MAÎTR-ISO is a workforce management platform built for construction companies in Quebec, Canada.

Many construction businesses still track attendance and hours with paper timesheets and phone calls, which leads to inaccurate records, payroll disputes and communication problems. Crews also move between job sites where the mobile network can be weak or missing entirely.

On top of that, companies must keep an eye on employee compliance documents such as CCQ cards and their expiry dates. The challenge was to deliver one centralized, bilingual construction management app that simplifies daily operations, keeps attendance records accurate, protects employee data and keeps working offline.

02

What We Built

We developed MAÎTR-ISO as complete workforce management software for the web and mobile, available in French and English (français et anglais), with four main user roles.

Company Owners

Run the company
  • Manage employees and job sites
  • Review working hours and attendance
  • Monitor compliance documents
  • Manage company operations

Foremen

Lead the crews
  • Manage construction crews
  • Organize shifts and schedules
  • Monitor job site activity
  • Create and review site reports

Employees

On the job site
  • Clock in and out at job sites
  • View schedules and recorded hours
  • Access and update their compliance documents
  • Stay connected with their team

Platform Administrators

Oversee the platform
  • Create and manage companies
  • Manage employees and attendance records
  • Monitor compliance information
  • Oversee platform operations

Key Features

A GPS attendance system with geofencing at its core, surrounded by employee scheduling software, compliance management and team communication.

01GPS-Based Attendance

  • GPS-enabled punch in and punch out
  • Geofencing so employees clock in only inside authorized job site areas
  • Location-based attendance verification

02Offline Functionality

  • Punches are saved on the device when there is no internet connection
  • Records sync automatically when connectivity returns
  • The original punch time is preserved

03Job Site & Workforce Management

  • Create and manage construction sites
  • Set each site's location and permitted GPS radius
  • Assign employees to job sites
  • Manage workforce allocation

04Scheduling & Hours Tracking

  • Calendar-based shift planning
  • Employee scheduling
  • Working-hour summaries
  • Attendance records by employee and job site

05Employee Compliance Management

  • SIN information
  • CCQ card numbers and expiry dates
  • Cheque specimen records
  • Automatic complete / incomplete compliance status

06Reports & Team Communication

  • Job site reports
  • Crew Feed with posts and comments
  • @mentions to notify teammates
  • Internal team communication

07Notifications & Access Control

  • In-app notifications for attendance, reports, compliance updates and mentions
  • Role-based permissions for owners, foremen and employees
  • Secure access to the right company information

08Multi-Company Management

  • Multiple companies on one platform
  • Separate data for each company
  • Users can work with more than one company
  • Admin and Superadmin panels

09Public Website

  • Public landing page
  • Customer contact form
  • Product introduction for potential customers
